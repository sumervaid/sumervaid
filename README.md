# Sumer S. Vaid

AI research scientist, builder and computational behavioral scientist. Postdoctoral fellow at the AI Institute, Harvard Business School. Stanford Ph.D.

Website: [sumervaid.com](https://sumervaid.com) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=vaamhuwAAAAJ) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sumer-vaid-ph-d-93ba1522/) · [CV](https://sumervaid.com/Sumer_Vaid_CV.pdf)

**Current questions.** When does a group of AI agents become misaligned together, and what stops the spread? Do LLM personas change from day to day the way people do? What does a measurement-theoretic view add to mechanistic interpretability?

**Open source**

- [PsyDyBench](https://github.com/sumervaid/PsyDyBench): a psychometric benchmark for the model psychology of multi-day LLM personas, 36 models from 9 developers. AI for Measurement Science Workshop @ COLM 2026.
- [MOSAIC Atlas](https://github.com/sumervaid/mosaic-atlas): open research infrastructure for causal identification across levels of analysis in agentic organizations. Clamp, ablate or steer inside agents, fork a team, compare branches.
- [Lemma](https://github.com/sumervaid/lemma): a type system for organizational constructs and propositions on Lean 4, with an AI pipeline that formalizes papers and a scorer that ranks propositions by need for evidence.
- [Everval](https://github.com/sumervaid/everval-ai): on-premises continuous benchmarking for AI models and agents, built from a repository's own history.
- [Reflex](https://github.com/sumervaid/reflex-tui): turns repeated agent sessions into skills and memory, and routes each task to a model by past sessions.
- [CEO](https://github.com/sumervaid/ceo): a terminal orchestrator for teams of headless AI agents with budgets, autonomy levels and a hash-chained ledger.
